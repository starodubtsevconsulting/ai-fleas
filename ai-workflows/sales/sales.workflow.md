# Sales workflow

Use this workflow for explicitly requested sales or lead-development work that moves an opportunity signal toward a
qualified commercial outcome while preserving evidence, relationship history, and deliberate strategy.

## Required behavior

- Distinguish known facts, inferred fit, assumptions, and unresolved qualification questions.
- Preserve provenance for research and relationship history.
- Do not fabricate familiarity, endorsements, customer evidence, urgency, or personalization.
- Treat contact details, correspondence, commercial terms, and relationship context as private profile-scoped data.
- Drafting outreach does not authorize sending it. Require explicit human authorization before any external message,
  CRM mutation, meeting booking, proposal delivery, or commercial commitment.
- Respect opt-outs, applicable contact restrictions, and profile-defined communication policy.
- Record outcomes without turning speculation into durable fact.

Typical flow: `opportunity -> research -> qualify -> choose strategy -> draft -> review -> human authorization -> contact -> record evidence -> follow up or close`.

The selected profile defines offers, segments, qualification policy, systems, identities, and permitted channels. The
host supplies CRM and communication mechanics without broadening authority.
