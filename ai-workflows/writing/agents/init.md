# Writing endpoint startup

Writing endpoints are direct human-created tasks. Create the declared roles in the selected Writing project with their
role contract and configured model settings. Binding receipts, INIT audits, lifecycle permits, hidden routing, and host
project-ID reconciliation are optional automation infrastructure and never prerequisites for direct endpoint use.

Endpoint creation never grants publication authority. A directly human-created Writer, Reviewer, or Release Coordinator
may perform its declared role immediately; it must not block merely because `*_READY` or a lifecycle receipt is absent.
