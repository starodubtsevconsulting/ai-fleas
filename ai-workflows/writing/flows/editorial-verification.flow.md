# Editorial verification flow

## Purpose

[Writing](../writing.workflow.md), step 3. Verify the article candidate before archiving and destination formatting.

## Entry

An editable candidate, source trail, brief, asset list, and target destination if known.

## Steps

1. The active writing task compares consequential factual claims, quotations, and links with their sources; proof:
   corrected text or an explicit unresolved-claim list. Separate fact, interpretation, and opinion.
2. The task checks title/subtitle against the [article headline guide](../guides/article-headlines.md), then checks
   opening, paragraph flow, heading hierarchy, lists, emphasis, code, attribution, and the author's voice. Make a
   separate idea-level repetition pass across sections: for each recurring point, ask whether it adds evidence,
   contrast, consequence, or an intentional callback. Combine or cut repetitions that merely restate a claim, but
   preserve refrains that earn their place; proof: a readable revised candidate and a brief disposition of flagged
   repetitions.
3. The task verifies each visual's meaning, legibility, source, and rights. For diagrams, preserve editable source
   and prepare a rendered image or a clear text fallback; proof: asset references and visual-review notes.
4. The task records passed, not-applicable, or blocked checks using the
   [shared evidence rules](../../_common/flows/contract.md#evidence-and-recovery); proof: a candidate-specific
   editorial result.

## Exit

Continue to [archive](archive.flow.md) when required checks pass. Failed checks return to
[drafting](drafting.flow.md) with the specific correction; preserve valid evidence and do not claim an unchecked
candidate is ready.
