# Session-scoped release authorization

A human may authorize one bounded Writing run once. Admin records the authorizing human message, current task, parent
correlation, article class or exact source, destination, permitted release action, target rule, and timing rule. That
authorization remains effective for every child stage in the same Admin-owned run. Agents must not ask the human to
repeat it merely because work moved between specialists or a review correction produced a new hash.

An instruction such as “write any test article, review it, and schedule it on Medium one week from now” delegates
selection of the final revision to the declared Writing review loop. It satisfies final-article acceptance when:

- Admin links the instruction to one parent correlation and preserved human message;
- the final revision stays within the authorized article class, account, destination, action, and timing envelope;
- every material correction is independently re-reviewed and the final Reviewer disposition is `ACCEPTABLE`;
- no unresolved factual, rights, destination, or safety defect remains; and
- Admin records the exact final hash selected under the mandate before release.

A new human decision is required when the article purpose, destination account, release action, target, timing rule,
or parent run changes; when a materially different article falls outside the authorized class; or when the human
revokes or narrows the mandate. An in-scope correction does not invalidate the mandate after renewed review.

Publication target remains explicit, but it need not be repeated. A target named once in the run stays bound. When
the human ordered release to the configured Medium account after being shown that it has no Publications and only its
author profile/home is eligible, Admin may record that sole verified target as the resolved meaning of the existing
order. This resolution is forbidden when multiple eligible targets exist or the limitation was not shown to the human.

Listen-through is a review aid, not a second release authorization. Record whether it was completed, declined,
unavailable, or rejected by the host. A failed optional narration does not trigger repeated consent requests or block
an otherwise valid session mandate unless the human made listening a condition of release.

Release Coordinator verifies the preserved mandate and final review evidence from Admin's canonical packet. It does
not ask for the same approval again and never expands the recorded envelope.
