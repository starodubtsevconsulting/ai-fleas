# Minimalist editorial realism

Reusable generated-image style for article headers and editorial illustrations.

## Intent

Communicate one clear editorial idea without the generic cinematic/AI-poster look.

## Visual direction

- One dominant visual story and obvious focal relationship.
- Few purposeful objects; remove decorative clutter.
- Substantial breathing room and simple spatial hierarchy.
- Realistic/editorial rather than promotional or fantastical presentation.
- Natural or subdued light; avoid automatic golden-hour drama, neon spectacle, exaggerated depth-of-field, heroic staging, and other default cinematic cues unless the subject requires them.
- Prefer ordinary, believable environments and objects over symbolic overload.

## Text

Default to no generated text anywhere in the image.

Do not add decorative writing to props, cups, books, clothing, screens, signs, walls, backgrounds, or equipment. Visible text is acceptable only when it is integral to the article concept or explicitly requested by the human; then it must satisfy the canonical header-image text checks.

## Use

Profiles may select this style by reference as their default without copying it. It complements, and does not replace, the canonical `guides/header-image-contract.md`.
