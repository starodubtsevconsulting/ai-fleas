# Lyrics or script writing flow

Executed by `@lyrics-script-worker`.

## Purpose

Turn an idea into approved lyrics, a spoken script, or narration while preserving the intended message and voice.

## Flow

1. Resolve the idea, format, audience, message, language, tone, and relevant constraints.
2. Draft the lyrics, script, or narration.
3. Review structure, voice, pacing, originality, and instruction following.
4. Revise with the human until approved.
5. Deliver the approved text to the Audio flow.

## Commands

- [`writing`](../../../ai-commands/writing/)

## Output

Approved lyrics, script, or narration, including the decisions the Audio flow must preserve.
