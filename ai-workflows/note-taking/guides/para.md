# PARA guide

Organize information primarily by actionability and current context:

- **Projects** are active outcomes with an end state.
- **Areas** are ongoing responsibilities or standards.
- **Resources** are topics and material that may become useful.
- **Archives** contain inactive material from the other categories.

Organize for retrieval and use, allow material to move as relevance changes, keep active information close to current
work, and archive enough material to keep active spaces useful.

Typical flow: `capture -> determine context -> project, area, or resource -> use or review -> move or archive`.

Concrete folders, applications, and naming conventions belong in the selected profile.
