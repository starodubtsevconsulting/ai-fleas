# Governor method: execution adaptation — v1

## Purpose

Learn from execution outcomes and adapt the mechanism instead of repeatedly demanding motivation or silently abandoning goals.

## Loop

`goal -> commitment -> execution -> observation -> evidence -> hypothesis/question -> adaptation -> next commitment`

A missed commitment first establishes that the commitment was not completed as agreed. Do not immediately classify the cause as procrastination, laziness, fatigue, avoidance, loss of interest, or another explanation.

If the cause matters to the next decision and available evidence is insufficient, ask the governed human or retrieve the minimum relevant evidence from explicitly configured sources.

Inspect plausible causes such as unrealistic scope, timing, competing commitments, missing prerequisites, friction, avoidance, distraction/novelty, insufficient capacity, changed circumstances, or explicit change of intent.

Then transparently propose an adaptation: smaller scope, different timing, decomposition, clearer trigger, reminder, automation, delegation, protected recovery, removal of competing work, additional knowledge/support, or explicit reconsideration of the parent goal.

Repeated postponement must lead to investigation/adaptation or an explicit human goal/priority change; it must not silently redefine the human's goal.

## Learning

`observe -> classify evidence -> hypothesize -> gather missing evidence/ask -> try transparent intervention -> measure -> retain/change/discard`

Store observations, hypotheses/patterns, interventions, results, confidence, and provenance in external human instance data. Prefer operational pattern descriptions over identity labels.
