# Personal Governor method: Actual activity reconciliation — v1

## Purpose

Keep planning evidence honest when the governed human's real activity diverges from the calendar, including periods
where the human directs AI work while simultaneously doing something else.

The method distinguishes **human activity** from **Governor/agent activity**. Agent execution is evidence about delegated
work; it is not automatically evidence that the human spent the same time actively working.

## Trigger

Apply when conversation, workflow evidence, or direct human report shows a material activity block or meaningful
departure from the current plan. Do not create calendar noise for brief questions, incidental chat, or tiny agent
actions.

## Procedure

1. Establish the human activity first: what the human is physically/attentionally doing, with a bounded time/window and
   confidence when known.
2. Separately establish Governor/agent activity: delegated implementation, research, PR creation, scheduling, memory
   maintenance, or other work performed on the human's behalf.
3. Detect concurrency. A human may mow a lawn, travel, rest, or perform another task while intermittently directing an
   agent. Do not convert the full elapsed agent execution window into human active-work time.
4. Compare actual human activity with current calendar commitments.
5. Preserve externally fixed commitments. For a displaced flexible/Governor-created block, move, shorten, replace, or
   cancel it when authorized and useful. Otherwise add/correct an actual-activity record only when it materially
   improves the calendar/evidence picture.
6. Record planned-versus-actual explicitly. Calendar remains the current scheduling surface; durable daily evidence
   preserves what actually happened and relevant outcomes.
7. Persist material Governor/agent outcomes separately when they matter to strategy/project history.

## Evidence shape

A reconciled period may contain:

- human activity: category, summary, start/end/window, source/confidence;
- concurrent delegated activity: agent/workflow, requested outcome, result/status;
- calendar relationship: matched, displaced, concurrent, unplanned, rescheduled, or cancelled;
- human attention mode when useful: primary, intermittent, supervisory, or unknown;
- material outcome/evidence references.

## Example

```text
13:00–14:00
Human: chalet yard work / mowing grass (primary physical activity)
Human involvement in AI-Fleas: intermittent supervisory decisions
Governor/agents: implemented Financial Insights PRs concurrently
Calendar: optional image experiment displaced
```

The correct record is not “human worked on Financial Insights for one hour.” The agent's throughput and the human's
time expenditure are different facts.

## Boundaries

- Never infer multitasking merely because agent work occurred during a human activity; record concurrency only when
  evidence supports it.
- Do not overwrite fixed/shared calendar events to make history look tidy.
- Do not treat an agent-created artifact as proof of equal human effort.
- Corrections should preserve history/provenance rather than silently rewrite evidence.
