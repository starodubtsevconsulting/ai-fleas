# Personal Governor method: Goal rehearsal — v1

## Purpose

Keep human-owned goals active, understood, communicable, and open to deliberate refinement rather than merely stored in memory.

Goal rehearsal is not a test of obedience or exact wording. The Governor uses recall and explanation to detect ambiguity, stale understanding, drift, or a useful change in how the human understands a goal.

## Inputs

Use only what is needed:

- active human-owned goals and current strategy;
- the human's own current explanation;
- recent evidence that may materially affect a goal;
- configured languages when multilingual rehearsal is useful.

## Procedure

### 1. Select a small scope

Choose one or a small number of important active goals. Do not rehearse the complete goal store by default.

### 2. Ask for recall before playback

Ask the human to state the selected goal in their own words before showing the stored formulation when practical.

Useful prompts include:

- What are you trying to accomplish?
- Why does it matter?
- How would you explain it to another person?
- What would progress toward it look like now?

Do not require memorized wording.

### 3. Compare meaning

Compare the human's explanation with the durable goal/strategy.

Distinguish:

- equivalent wording;
- clearer articulation;
- incomplete or ambiguous understanding;
- genuine goal drift;
- a deliberate new insight that may justify updating the goal.

Do not treat wording differences as misalignment.

### 4. Clarify or refine

When useful, help the human produce a concise formulation they can naturally explain.

If the rehearsal exposes a material change in intent, do not silently overwrite durable strategy. Confirm that the human intends to change it, then use normal memory/governance semantics.

### 5. Optional multilingual rehearsal

When the profile identifies languages the human is actively developing or must use, the Governor may ask for the same goal to be explained naturally in one of those languages.

The objective is practical articulation, not literal translation or language examination. Correct only enough to improve useful communication unless the human asks for deeper language coaching.

### 6. Record only durable change

Do not persist every rehearsal transcript. Record only material clarification, deliberate goal changes, or strategically useful evidence.

## Cadence

Cadence is profile-configured. Prefer occasional rehearsal over repetitive recitation. The Governor may also invoke the method when evidence suggests the human cannot clearly explain an important goal or when a goal has materially changed.

## Output

A rehearsal should normally end with one of:

- goal remains understood; no durable change;
- clearer formulation captured;
- ambiguity identified for follow-up;
- human-approved goal/strategy update;
- practical multilingual formulation improved.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- turning rehearsal into a loyalty or compliance test;
- requiring verbatim repetition;
- drilling every goal every day;
- treating imperfect language as goal misalignment;
- rewriting a human-owned goal without confirmation;
- optimizing presentation while ignoring whether the underlying goal still makes sense.
