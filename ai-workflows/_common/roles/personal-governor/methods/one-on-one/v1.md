# Governor method: one-on-one — v1

## Purpose

Provide proactive qualitative evidence through a direct Governor–human conversation rather than waiting for metrics, missed commitments, or explicit problems.

A Governor method may contain a procedure when the activity is naturally repeatable. This does not make it a workflow `flow`; workflow flows remain an operational workflow concept.

## Trigger

Run at the cadence configured by the profile/strategy, or on demand when a meaningful review is justified.

## Procedure

1. Review active goals, priorities, and material changes since the previous review.
2. Review commitments and relevant execution evidence.
3. Review recent external feedback when it materially affects active goals.
4. Review hot/fact-memory summaries when configured.
5. Review recent permanent-memory activity when configured.
6. Inspect unprocessed fleeting notes/inbox state and identify material worth reviewing with the human.
7. Discuss alignment, friction, perceived progress, changing circumstances, and whether current Governor guidance is helping.
8. Propose transparent adaptations to commitments, guidance, strategy assumptions, or memory organization.
9. With applicable authority/confirmation, promote useful fleeting material to permanent knowledge, retain references, or discard obsolete/trivial capture.
10. Agree on the next credible commitments and persist durable conclusions.
11. Refresh/update fact/index memory when the configured memory adapter supports it.

The conversation should remain relatively free. The procedure provides continuity and coverage, not a performance interrogation script.

## Useful discussion areas

- whether active goals still represent what the human wants;
- what feels effective or ineffective;
- recurring friction or avoidance;
- whether commitments and schedules are realistic;
- whether the Governor's current guidance is helping;
- missing knowledge, skills, tools, routines, or support;
- useful discoveries that may be trapped in fleeting notes or recent activity;
- opportunities the quantitative evidence may not reveal.

## Output

Persist only durable conclusions supported by evidence or explicitly confirmed by the human. Distinguish subjective evidence from objective execution evidence. Propose changes transparently and let the human confirm strategic changes.

## Configuration

Cadence is profile-owned. The method does not hard-code weekly, daily, or another schedule. A strategy may recommend a default cadence, while the concrete Governor profile selects the operational schedule.
