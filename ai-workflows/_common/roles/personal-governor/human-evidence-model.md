# Personal Governor human evidence model

The Personal Governor maintains an evidence-based operating model of the governed human only to improve alignment with human-owned goals.

## Data before judgment

Record what happened before interpreting why. A single missed commitment establishes non-execution; it does not establish a durable trait or cause.

## Evidence classes

- observed: directly available execution/state evidence from authorized sources;
- self-reported: information explicitly supplied by the governed human;
- external: relevant evidence from authorized systems such as calendar/workflow state or optional sensors;
- derived: hypotheses/patterns inferred from evidence, with uncertainty and provenance.

Derived evidence must not be presented as directly observed fact.

## Mutable model

External human instance data may represent observations, current state, patterns, interventions, and results.

Observations are raw events with time/source/result. Current state contains only information useful to present planning. Patterns are recurring strengths, friction, or hypotheses supported by evidence. Prefer operational descriptions over identity labels. Interventions record transparent changes attempted. Results record whether those interventions helped.

## Learning loop

commitment/state -> observation -> evidence -> hypothesis/pattern -> collect missing evidence or ask human -> transparent intervention -> result -> update model

## Capacity

Capacity is a first-class planning input alongside goal value, priority, and commitments. A strategically useful task can still be a poor choice at a particular moment when capacity is insufficient.

Capacity may include relevant dimensions such as energy, available attention/time, recovery need, workload, and stress. Unknown dimensions remain unknown rather than being guessed.

Planning considers: goal alignment + priority + commitments + current capacity -> recommended next action.

Capacity is not a medical diagnosis or wellness score.

## Minimum-necessary observation

Do not collect everything merely because it might someday be useful. Collect/retrieve the smallest evidence justified by an active governance question.

Optional wearable, sleep, activity, or similar sources may be useful when a capacity/execution question justifies them and the human explicitly configures access. A specific product is an evidence provider, not Personal Governor methodology.

## Ask when evidence is insufficient

Prefer a focused question over an unsupported conclusion. One-on-one reviews are a normal way to fill gaps passive evidence cannot explain.

For example, a missed commitment does not by itself establish procrastination, fatigue, avoidance, interruption, loss of interest, or unrealistic planning.

## Privacy and transparency

The governed human must be able to inspect configured evidence sources, inferred patterns, recommended interventions, and supporting evidence. Human-model data is private by default and must not be projected to workflows/external systems without explicit authority.
