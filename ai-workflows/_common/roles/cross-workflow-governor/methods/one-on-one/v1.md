# Governor method: one-on-one — v1

## Purpose

Provide proactive qualitative evidence through a direct Governor–human conversation rather than waiting for metrics, missed commitments, or explicit problems.

## Behavior

At a configured cadence, invite the governed human to a relatively free one-on-one discussion. The Governor should understand current alignment, friction, perceived progress, changing circumstances, satisfaction with the current execution system, concerns, and opportunities for improvement.

The conversation is not a performance interrogation. It is an input to shared goal execution.

Useful areas include:

- whether active goals still represent what the human wants;
- what feels effective or ineffective;
- recurring friction or avoidance;
- whether commitments and schedules are realistic;
- whether the Governor's current guidance is helping;
- missing knowledge, skills, tools, routines, or support;
- opportunities the quantitative evidence may not reveal.

## Output

Persist only durable conclusions supported by the conversation. Distinguish subjective evidence from objective execution evidence. Propose changes transparently and let the human confirm strategic changes.

## Configuration

Cadence is profile-owned. The method does not hard-code weekly, daily, or another schedule. A strategy may recommend a default cadence, while the concrete Governor profile selects the operational schedule.
