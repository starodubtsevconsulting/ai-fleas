# Governor Strategy and Workflow Governance

## Purpose

Portable human-readable template for the Governor's **goal and workflow side**. It owns strategic direction, priorities, allocation, evidence, and governance across workflows. It must not contain the detailed behavioral model of the governed human; that belongs in `human.template.md`.

Storage is intentionally unspecified. Markdown, Google Docs, a database, or a memory adapter may realize this structure as long as the semantics remain available to the Governor and inspectable by the governed human.

## Workflow allocation

For each relevant workflow:

- workflow reference
- goals supported
- current allocation / attention
- expected contribution
- constraints
- current evidence / state

Workflow awareness does not grant the Governor ordinary workflow-execution authority.

## Decision policy

Record reusable principles for comparing opportunities and allocating scarce attention. Keep these portable and goal-oriented rather than implementation-specific.

## Decisions

For each material decision:

- decision
- context
- rationale
- alternatives considered
- evidence
- opportunity cost
- confidence
- owner
- review trigger
- result when known

## Hypotheses and opportunities

Keep speculative possibilities separate from active goals. A conversation, mood, or interesting idea does not become strategy merely because it appeared.

## Risks and constraints

For each material risk or constraint:

- description
- impact / likelihood when useful
- mitigation
- escalation or review trigger

## Evidence

Keep strategic evidence sufficient to change allocation or goals. Prefer real outcomes over activity signals.

## Review cadence

Define on-demand, periodic, and event-triggered strategic reviews. Reviews should answer what matters now, what changed, what should be deferred, and whether allocation should change.

## Authority

The governed human owns the goals and may change, pause, override, or cancel them. External effects remain subject to the role contract and profile-authorized capabilities.

## Cross-link

The human-side model is a separate component. Link it here, but do not duplicate traits, execution history, behavioral hypotheses, or intervention strategy into this document.
