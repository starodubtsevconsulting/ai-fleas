# Memory method: Zettelkasten-inspired — v1

## Purpose

Define a portable human-readable permanent-memory organization that can be used manually, semi-manually, or by authorized agents. The permanent store is the governed human's durable knowledge layer; optimized fact/index memory may sit above it for fast machine retrieval.

## Knowledge lifecycle

`capture -> review -> discard | reference | promote to permanent concept | compose into output`

## Default semantic areas

- **Inbox** — fleeting observations and temporary capture awaiting review.
- **References** — literature/source/reference notes with provenance.
- **Concepts** — durable, self-contained permanent notes expressed in the human's own knowledge system and linked to related ideas.
- **Outputs** — composed artifacts such as articles, presentations, videos, specifications, or other products derived from knowledge.

Physical folder names are adapter/storage concerns. An Obsidian vault, Google Drive hierarchy, files, or another store may realize the same semantics.

## Metadata/index separation

Permanent content should remain human-readable and durable. Search/index metadata may be stored separately and link back to permanent objects. The metadata/index layer may contain ids, types, lifecycle state, topics, relationships, provenance, timestamps, activity, summaries, and retrieval hints.

This permits a hot/fact-memory implementation to change independently of the permanent knowledge store.

## Review

Fleeting notes are temporary capture, not permanent knowledge. Review them periodically and decide whether to discard, preserve as reference, promote into durable concepts, or use them in an output.

Do not preserve material merely because it exists. Preserve information that remains useful, meaningful, evidential, or connected to active/likely future reasoning.

## Human use

The governed human may use this method without the Governor. The Governor may inspect and maintain the memory only through profile-authorized memory capabilities.
