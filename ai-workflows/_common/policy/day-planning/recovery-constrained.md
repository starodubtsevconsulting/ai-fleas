# Recovery-constrained overlay

Reusable Personal Governor day-planning overlay for a day when current recovery/capacity evidence is materially below the person's normal planning assumptions.

This is an overlay, not a new goal or permanent strategy. It changes today's allocation while preserving the human's underlying goals.

## Trigger

Apply only when current evidence justifies it, for example unusually short/poor sleep, clearly reduced subjective readiness, illness/recovery needs, or another profile-authorized capacity signal.

Do not infer the trigger from a single wearable metric when the evidence is ambiguous.

## Rules

1. Preserve essential commitments and the highest-value bounded work where feasible.
2. Reduce expected cognitive load and total demanding-work allocation.
3. Add or protect recovery opportunities such as sleep, quiet rest, walking, meals, and low-load transitions as appropriate.
4. Make demanding blocks conditional on reassessment when capacity may change during the day.
5. Prefer concrete bounded tasks over exploratory work that can expand indefinitely.
6. Do not attempt to recover lost productive hours by extending serious work into protected evening/sleep boundaries.
7. Avoid treating temporary fatigue as evidence that long-term goals should change.
8. Record enough evidence to determine whether the condition is isolated or recurring.

## Exit

The overlay applies to the current planning horizon only. Return to the selected base day-planning strategy when recovery/capacity evidence no longer supports the constraint. Repeated activation should trigger review of the profile's baseline schedule, sleep boundaries, workload, or other relevant planning assumptions.
