# Methodology references

The initial day-planning strategy intentionally uses established ideas rather than presenting them as an AI Fleas invention.

## Time blocking

Time blocking assigns work to explicit blocks of time rather than relying only on an open-ended task list. The `fixed-schedule-time-blocking` strategy uses this as its scheduling mechanism.

A commonly known modern practitioner and writer on time-block planning is Cal Newport. See his published discussion of time-block planning and fixed-schedule productivity for background. AI Fleas does not require or reproduce any proprietary planner format.

## Fixed-schedule productivity

The strategy also uses a predetermined boundary for serious work. Work is fitted within that boundary rather than assuming unfinished work should automatically extend the day.

## Recovery and sleep

AI Fleas treats recovery evidence as a planning constraint rather than a productivity methodology. The `recovery-constrained` overlay deliberately avoids diagnosing health or deriving readiness from one wearable number. It modifies allocation only when the available evidence supports doing so.

## Design rule

Named external methodologies are background references, not authorities over the governed human. The human's explicit goals and constraints remain authoritative, and the Governor adapts planning from observed evidence.
