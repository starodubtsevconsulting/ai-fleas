# Fixed-schedule time-blocking

Reusable Personal Governor day-planning strategy.

## Intent

Translate active goals, commitments, available time, and human capacity into a bounded day rather than an unbounded task list.

This strategy combines time blocking with a fixed end to serious work. A profile supplies personal parameters; the strategy supplies the reusable planning rules.

## Rules

1. Protect sleep and recovery as constraints on the plan, not leftover time after work.
2. Establish explicit wake, serious-work-stop, electronics/shutdown, and target-sleep boundaries when the profile provides them.
3. Allocate the highest-quality expected energy to the highest-priority goal that benefits from it.
4. Time-block important work. Do not assume every unscheduled hour is productive capacity.
5. Include genuine breaks, meals, movement, household/family obligations, and transition time where relevant.
6. Re-plan the remaining day when reality invalidates the schedule instead of treating the original calendar as a failure.
7. Do not compensate for a missed block by automatically extending the workday or consuming protected sleep.
8. Prefer a small number of meaningful blocks over filling the calendar with low-value activity.
9. Preserve the distinction between strategic-goal work, obligations/maintenance, recovery, and discretionary activity.
10. The human can override any block or boundary. Repeated overrides are evidence for adapting the plan or profile, not grounds for coercion.

## Daily planning loop

Observe current commitments and capacity -> protect hard boundaries -> allocate primary-goal blocks -> allocate obligations -> add recovery/transitions -> leave useful slack -> execute -> re-plan when necessary -> review evidence.

## Profile parameters

Profiles may configure values such as:

- target wake time
- serious-work stop time
- electronics/shutdown time
- target sleep time
- preferred primary-goal block/daypart
- maximum or target deep-work capacity
- recurring household/family obligations
- recovery rules or overlays

Exact clock times belong to the profile, not this reusable strategy.

## Evidence and adaptation

The Governor should compare planned blocks with actual execution and relevant capacity evidence over time. Repeated evidence may justify changing profile parameters or selecting another strategy. A single disrupted day should not silently rewrite the strategy.
