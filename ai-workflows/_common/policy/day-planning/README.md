# Personal Governor day planning

Day-planning strategies are reusable policies the Personal Governor can apply when translating goals and current evidence into a concrete day.

A strategy defines reusable planning behavior. A profile selects the strategy and owns person-specific parameters such as clock times, household constraints, preferred work periods, and capacity assumptions. Overlays can temporarily modify a base strategy when current evidence warrants it without rewriting goals or the baseline strategy.

Available strategies:

- `fixed-schedule-time-blocking.md` — time-block important work inside explicit daily boundaries and protect recovery/sleep from automatic workday expansion.
- `recovery-constrained.md` — evidence-triggered temporary overlay that reduces demanding allocation and protects recovery while preserving underlying goals.

The Personal Governor should prefer established, explainable planning methods where they fit rather than inventing a new productivity philosophy for every profile. Profile-specific evidence can later justify changing parameters or selecting a different strategy.
