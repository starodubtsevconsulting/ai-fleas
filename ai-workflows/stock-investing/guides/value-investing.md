# Value-investing guide

Treat a stock as an ownership interest in a business and require a defensible gap between estimated value and market
price to account for uncertainty and analytical error.

- Understand the business, industry, economics, competitive position, and major risks.
- Estimate a range of value from appropriate fundamental evidence rather than price momentum alone.
- State the assumptions and margin-of-safety requirement.
- Investigate why an apparently inexpensive security is cheap.
- Distinguish temporary pessimism from deterioration in business value.
- Revisit the thesis when material evidence changes rather than reacting automatically to ordinary volatility.
- Judge process quality separately from short-term outcomes.

Valuation methods, concentration limits, eligible securities, and execution permissions are profile-specific and must
not be inferred from this guide.
