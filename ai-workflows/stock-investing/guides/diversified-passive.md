# Diversified-passive-investing guide

Prefer a simple, broadly diversified, low-cost, long-term portfolio over attempts to predict individual winners or
short-term market movements.

- Select an allocation based on human-supplied objectives, time horizon, risk capacity, and constraints.
- Contribute consistently rather than assuming market timing is reliable.
- Rebalance according to a reviewed policy, not recent headlines.
- Minimize unnecessary complexity, turnover, fees, taxes, and behavioral reactions.
- Change strategic allocation because objectives or constraints changed, not merely because prices moved.

Concrete instruments, percentages, accounts, tax treatment, and thresholds require profile-specific review and are not
implied by this public guide.
