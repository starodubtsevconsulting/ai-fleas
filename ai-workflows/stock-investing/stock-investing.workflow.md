# Stock-investing workflow

Use this workflow for explicitly requested long-horizon investment research and decision support. Preserve theses,
assumptions, evidence, valuation reasoning, risks, decisions, and outcomes so later review does not restart from market
noise.

## Strategy selection

- [Diversified passive investing](guides/diversified-passive.md) emphasizes broad diversification, low costs, and
  disciplined allocation.
- [Value investing](guides/value-investing.md) emphasizes company-level research, defensible valuation, and a margin of
  safety.

## Required behavior

- Separate observed facts, estimates, assumptions, scenarios, and conclusions.
- Preserve source provenance and the date or reporting period of market-sensitive evidence.
- State uncertainty, relevant risks, thesis invalidation conditions, and missing evidence.
- Do not claim guaranteed performance or treat historical results as assurance of future outcomes.
- Research and portfolio execution are separate capabilities. Never place, modify, or cancel an order, move money, or
  change an account without explicit authorization for the exact action and independently enforced limits.
- Keep holdings, account details, credentials, constraints, and execution permissions private and profile-scoped.
- Encourage qualified professional review when legal, tax, fiduciary, or suitability questions materially affect a
  decision.

Typical flow: `objective and constraints -> strategy -> evidence -> thesis and valuation -> risk review -> human decision -> optional separately authorized execution -> monitor -> retrospective`.
