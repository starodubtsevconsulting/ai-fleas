# Reconstruct evidence from account statements

## Purpose and entry

Use this reusable function when a bounded financial question needs evidence derived from bank/card statements, or when a primary report is missing, no usable schedule exists, or quarter reconciliation needs a source-backed statement view. The explicitly authorized organization folders are the system of record by design; no financial-software or payroll-register prerequisite applies. For example, it can reconstruct statement-supported salary cash payments to a particular employee; it cannot reconstruct gross payroll, deductions, or a payroll schedule. Statements may not identify every recipient, and the function does not promise that they always contain enough information to answer the question.

This is a read-only evidence method unless a separately authorized effect is explicitly supplied. It does not require full-quarter closure for a bounded question or create a new command/skill. For authorized salary reconstruction, retain one narrow durable derived projection at `<business>/qN/out/salary/salary-payments.json` per business quarter, when the current profile/task permits that persistence. This is the approved derived-view type for that use case, not primary evidence, a general extraction cache, receipt, or standing publication permission. The `out/` path groups salary by topic; projection metadata identifies the artifact as derived, non-posting, and non-additive. Do not create a PDF copy, CSV copy, second manifest, or other aggregate file. JSON-to-CSV export is on demand only.

Call it conceptually as `reconstruct_from_statements(question, scope, human_rules, target_employee?) -> evidence_result`. `target_employee` and an employee map are optional; this names a documentation function, not an executable command ID or a claim that a runtime adapter exists. A bounded lookup calls it as needed and returns its evidence in the answer. Quarter work invokes it from Collect when statement evidence must be obtained/processed, or from Reconcile when available statements need matching; it does not skip Inspect or the quarter's independent coverage checks.

## Function contract

**Inputs**

- the human's question and requested facts or measure;
- requested period and as-of date, if relevant;
- authorized accounts, businesses, currencies, sections, storage roots, and available tools;
- any human-supplied classification rules, including their source and effective date;
- optionally, the requested employee and an authorized employee map containing full names/aliases, masked-account matches, effective dates, and dated human classification rules;
- permitted effects, such as read-only analysis or an explicitly authorized update to the existing quarter summary.

Resolve authority and mapping before discovery. An employee map is optional and may be loaded only from the authorized organization root; keep it private. A recommended organization is an `employees.md` inside the organization's existing data folder, with the workflow/profile pointing to that authorized organization root. This is a location convention, not a request to create a file or a duplicate configuration store. If the map is absent, a narrow statement lookup can still return source-supported employee-specific matches and leave the rest unallocated or unresolved; missing employee metadata alone does not block that lookup. A classification or identity rule is usable only when the human explicitly supplied it or it is otherwise authorized, dated, and source-linked. A human confirmation can be recorded in the existing private quarter summary; no separate external formal record is required to use that confirmation within its stated scope. Similar descriptions, recurrence, a name alone, or an agent's intuition are not rules.

**Outputs**

- requested rows or facts linked to original statement paths and page/row references, with validated adjacent extraction identified separately;
- matched and excluded rows with rationale, retaining original date, direction, signed amount, currency, FX fields where present, and match links;
- cash subtotals separated by currency and by the requested classification/basis;
- for an employee-targeted question, employee-allocated amounts separately from salary amounts that remain unallocated to any employee;
- inspected account, statement-period, page/row and transaction coverage;
- unresolved or unmatched items, assumptions, and the first next action;
- independent result states for coverage/completeness, payment/settlement evidence, and classification, each marked supported, partial, unresolved, or not applicable as appropriate;
- an overall function state of `success`, `partial`, or `blocked`, plus the applicable review state and the evidence/limitation that determines each.

Do not return one blended cross-currency total unless the human requested a conversion basis and supported FX evidence supplies it. Preserve facts observed in the source separately from classifications applied by a dated human rule.

`success` means the requested statement-evidence scope is covered sufficiently to answer the bounded request; it does not imply full-quarter readiness, tax/accounting review, or quarter closure. `partial` returns supported rows with a material gap. `blocked` means authority, scope, or a required capability prevents dependent discovery. Payment/settlement evidence and classification remain independent outputs: a classified salary deposit does not prove a complete salary schedule, and a posted debit does not establish its tax liability or program.

## Method

1. **Bound the question.** Restate the requested measure, period, basis (for example posted cash), accounts, currencies, employee target if any, and required answer. Distinguish a transaction question from invoice revenue, tax liability, or other accrual question. Use only authorized roots and effects.
2. **Inventory relevant statements.** Inspect the existing authorized quarter summary and bounded account/statement inventory. Identify statement intervals, gaps, duplicate copies, and relevant adjacent periods needed for boundary transactions. Do not expand to unrelated accounts or perform a full-quarter audit for a narrow question.
3. **Read validated adjacent data first.** Reuse existing valid CSV/JSON sidecars according to their actual schema, recording exact source bindings and covered pages/rows. Then verify material candidate facts against original statement pages: dates, debit/credit direction, amounts, currencies, FX, references, and any field needed for the answer. An absent, stale, malformed, or conflicting sidecar returns the affected source to supported processing; filenames and sidecars alone do not prove a transaction.
4. **Reconstruct rows.** Scan all in-scope statement rows needed for coverage, not just familiar names or an expected recurring schedule. Preserve signed direction, transaction/posting dates as labeled, source account, original description, currency, and page/row provenance. Keep observed description and human classification in separate fields.
5. **Match and deduplicate.** Link internal transfers, card-balance payments, and related Wise legs when amount/currency/date/reference evidence supports the relationship. Represent each underlying economic movement once for the requested measure; retain both sides as linked evidence. Preserve unmatched or ambiguous legs as unresolved. Do not infer a transfer match from amount alone when competing candidates exist.
6. **Apply only valid classification rules.** Apply an explicit, dated, source-linked human rule within its stated scope. For an employee-targeted total, allocate an outgoing payment only when the statement evidence links it to that employee or an authorized, effective-dated map/human rule links its name or masked account match. A recipient name alone does not prove that an account is personal or that a payment has a particular purpose. Recurrence is a candidate-finding signal, not proof of salary, rent, refund, tax, employee identity, or an expected amount. Salary payments that lack employee attribution remain in an organization salary-unallocated subtotal when their salary classification is supported; do not drop them or attribute them to the requested employee. Keep owner, vendor, refund, and tax-settlement assignments pending when evidence or the human rule is missing.
7. **Calculate bounded results.** Sum signed integer-cent amounts within each currency and requested category after matching, exclusions, reversals, and duplicates are represented. For an employee-targeted salary question, show the verified employee subtotal and any salary-unallocated subtotal separately. Keep fees and FX separate when the source shows them. Do not infer gross income, deductions, invoice revenue, taxable income, tax liability, or tax settlement from bank amounts alone. A tax debit may evidence posted cash while its tax program/liability remains unresolved.
8. **Return evidence and stop.** Report source-linked rows, per-currency subtotals, coverage, unresolved matches/classifications, and review state. State whether material fields were read mechanically or checked visually. Do not represent a partial subtotal as a complete amount. For ordinary authorized persistence, update only the existing mapped quarter summary. The separately approved salary projection below is the sole durable row-level exception.

## Approved salary-payment projection

For authorized salary-payment reconstruction, the approved projection path above records statement-supported cash payments and classification decisions; it is not a payroll register, payroll liability, receipt, accounting posting, or additive expense source. Writing it remains subject to the exact profile scope and persistence authorization for the current task. Keep all monetary values as signed decimal strings in their original currency. Do not infer gross payroll, deductions, tax treatment, or liabilities.

The JSON object uses this contract (field names and types are normative):

```json
{
  "schema_version": "statement-salary-projection.v1",
  "artifact_type": "statement_salary_payments",
  "derived": true,
  "posting": false,
  "additive_expense": false,
  "source_path_base": "reports/2027",
  "generated_at": "2027-07-02",
```

`source_path_base` anchors all source and rule paths. `bindings.inventory_scope.roots` lists exact authorized source folders, `selection` defines which originals count as relevant, and `inventory_sha256` hashes the canonical sorted list of every relevant relative source path and PDF hash. Recheck the same roots and selection to detect newly added or removed sources; do not scan an unrelated annual tree. `page` is the verified original PDF page number. `row` is the source's actual printed row number only when one is present; otherwise use JSON `null`, and optionally set `locator_type` to a mechanical locator such as `text_occurrence`. Never invent a printed row number or page. A stable `payment_id` uses the source SHA-256, account, date and actual reference when present; if no reference exists, use the verified page, literal description, signed amount and occurrence identity. Classification wording or display order alone must not change it. If a material source binding or locator cannot be verified, retain the candidate only as pending and exclude it from subtotals until resolved. `employee_id: null` means salary is classified but not attributed to an employee; `salary_subtotal_inclusion` is one of `included`, `excluded`, or `pending`. `classification_status` distinguishes source-observed facts from dated human rules, while `match_pending` flags an unresolved transfer match. Reversals are separate signed rows linked by reference when supported. Pending Wise legs stay distinct until a source-supported match exists; never add or remove them from CAD subtotals through guessed FX. `bindings.inventory_scope` fixes the exact authorized business/quarter source folders and relevant adjacent-period boundary needed for the question; `sources` fingerprints every relevant inventoried original considered from that scope, including those excluded or deduplicated. `inventory_status` records its disposition; duplicate copies may include a `duplicate_of` payment/source ID and must not create another included row. `bindings.employee_mapping_sha256` is null when no authorized employee map was used. Each classification rule binds to its exact provenance path, confirmation/effective dates, and a canonical hash of only the selected rule and identity/alias content (not a hash of the entire quarter summary or other mutable container). A later edit to unrelated summary text must not stale the projection. When persistence is unavailable but an in-memory human instruction is authorized for the task, record its confirmation date and exact instruction provenance without fabricating a private rule file.

Include fingerprints and coverage for every relevant source considered in the quarter source inventory, not just files that contribute an included row. For duplicate files, retain their inventory fingerprint and a deduplication/link status, but use one logical source row in totals. Record account/currency intervals and explicit missing coverage. Outgoing statement rows retain negative `signed_amount` values; salary-paid subtotals report the positive magnitude of included outgoing rows by employee and currency, plus a separate unallocated salary-paid subtotal for each currency. The function and projection retain independent coverage, payment/matching, classification, and review states. A missing source, classification rule, mapping, or account interval yields a partial projection with supported rows retained and a precise limitation; do not fill a gap with zero or double-count a transfer.

Reuse the projection only if the bounded inventory scope and membership, every relevant source hash, account/currency coverage, canonical selected-rule content hash plus provenance/effective-date binding, and employee identity/mapping binding still match. Recheck the same mapped folders and quarter-boundary sources, not an unrelated annual tree. Compare only the selected dated rule and alias content; unrelated edits to the summary that contains it do not invalidate the projection. If all bindings are unchanged, reuse without rewriting. If something changed, mark the projection stale and refresh only affected rows through the authorized persistence route; do not silently rewrite history or treat this derivative as a historical source. The quarter summary may link to the projection and state its date/status and limitations, but must not duplicate its row ledger or full subtotals as a competing authority.

Any expense or cash-flow consumer must explicitly exclude this projection from additive ingestion (`additive_expense: false`) and link source transactions by stable identity. A payment represented by the statement and this projection is one cash movement, counted once. Payroll liabilities, gross pay, and paystub amounts are not new statement cash expenses. This explicit consumer rule prevents duplicate totals; it does not implement a runtime importer or claim that a financial-records command supports this projection.

## Blocked and resume behavior

If an authorized root/account, period, effect, or source cannot be established, block only the dependent work and name the missing scope or capability. If statement coverage is missing, extraction is invalid, a transfer match is ambiguous, a classification rule is absent, or a material source field cannot be verified, retain supported rows and mark the affected result partial or review-pending. Do not fill a gap with a schedule, recollection, or presumed zero.

Keep a compact checkpoint of the question/scope, exact statement paths and sidecars, completed intervals/pages, candidate and matched rows, classification rules and their sources/dates, unresolved items, and next action. On resume, verify changed scope/source bindings, then continue at the first unfinished batch; reuse unchanged validated evidence and do not recount already matched movements.

## Fictional acceptance scenarios

| Scenario | Expected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Employee-specific statement salary | The authorized folder contains a current, effective-dated employee map with aliases and a masked-account match. A named CAD 1,000 debit and a debit matching the employee's masked account total CAD 1,500 for that employee. A recurring unnamed CAD 500 debit follows a dated salary rule but remains salary-unallocated. A separate recalled CAD 500 payment to another employee has no matching row and is not added. Report employee-allocated and unallocated subtotals separately; do not infer gross payroll or require external payroll software. |
| Matching transfer legs | A personal account debit and a business account credit in USD have matching date, amount, currency, and a consistent transfer reference. Link the two legs and exclude them from income/spending totals that would count the underlying transfer twice. If a competing leg makes the match ambiguous, leave it pending. |
| Recipient is not classification | A named payee and recurring CAD 800 transfer do not establish that the destination is a personal account or owner draw. Keep the classification unresolved unless a valid, scoped human rule or stronger evidence supports it. |
| Broader record types | A refund is matched to its original purchase where supported; rent follows an explicit scope rule; a tax bank debit is reported as cash with tax program/liability unresolved absent supporting evidence. Never derive gross income, deductions, invoice/tax conclusions from bank amounts alone. |
| Missing statement interval | The available statement ends mid-period. Return verified rows and a per-currency subtotal as partial, name the exact interval/account gap, and resume collection or processing there. A balanced statement or expected monthly schedule does not close the gap. |
| Bounded question | The human asks for one account's posted CAD deposits in a date range. Return the supported rows, subtotal and coverage without requiring unrelated account inventory, full-quarter readiness, or quarter closure. |

These scenarios are invented instructional cases, not executed evidence or a guarantee that every classification can be resolved from statements.
