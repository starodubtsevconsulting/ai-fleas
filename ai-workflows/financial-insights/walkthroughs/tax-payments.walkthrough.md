# Worked use case: taxes paid to Quebec

Fictional instructional example; no files described here were executed or measured. These steps classify documentary evidence, not tax obligations or filing advice.

**Request:** “How much tax did I pay to Quebec in Q2 2027?”

**Short plan:** determine the authorized organization/accounts; use April 1–June 30 posted cash payments as the period; discover tax documents and bank statements; distinguish recipient, tax program, liability period, and payment status; reconcile confirmations to debits; return a supported amount with precise scope.

1. **Map the question to evidence.** Read applicable rules and the actual profile mapping. The fictional mapping has `2027/studio/Q2/out/taxes` and `2027/studio/Q2/statements`. Also inspect the mapped Q1 tax section because a Q1 liability may be paid during Q2, and adjacent statement periods if they cover Q2 transactions. Search tax confirmations and statement rows independently. Why: “paid in Q2” concerns the payment date, not the return's quarter, folder name, or document print date.
2. **Inventory and read structured records.** List the mapped sections in bounded batches. Pair PDFs with JSON without dropping PDFs that lack sidecars. Read recipient, program, liability dates, requested payment date, posted debit date, status, amount/currency, confirmation reference, and original source links. Extract missing/incomplete JSON through the configured read-only PDF/text tool into private evidence. These are conceptual operations; resolve actual tool names from the selected profile.
3. **Separate requests from settlements.** A fictional `q1-remittance-paid.pdf` shows “To be processed” and an April 12 requested payment. Its filename says paid, but that does not establish settlement. A bank statement has an April 12 debit of CAD 840.00 with the same reference. Match them: this is one confirmed Q2 payment for a Q1 liability. Another confirmation requests CAD 841.00 on July 14 for Q2; its declaration components total CAD 840.00. Preserve that discrepancy and exclude the scheduled July payment from Q2 cash paid. Do not force the form and confirmation to agree or overwrite either value.
4. **Check recipient, program, and direction.** A payee label alone cannot identify the tax program. Keep the payee/jurisdiction separate from GST/QST, income tax, payroll remittance, or unknown program. A Quebec-administered GST/QST payment must retain its program identity; do not relabel every dollar as provincial income tax. Payroll remittances may include contributions and require their documented breakdown. The statement also contains “GOUV QUEBEC” CAD 50.00. Extracted text lost its column alignment: inspect the original table page and observe that it is in the deposit column. Record a government credit with purpose unknown; do not assume it is a tax refund or subtract it from remittances. A federal debit and a municipal debit are separately classified, even though the municipality is geographically in Quebec.

A matching reference supports the link; investigate conflicts rather than assuming identical amounts alone prove identity.
