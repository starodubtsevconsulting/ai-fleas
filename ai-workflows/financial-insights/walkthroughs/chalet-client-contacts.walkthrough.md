# Worked use case: chalet clients and contact information

Fictional instructional example; names, contacts, paths, dates, and observations below are invented, not executed evidence.

**Request:** “Extract all chalet clients and their contact information for Q2 2027.”

**Plan:** resolve the authorized chalet reservation sources; establish which stays belong to the quarter; extract lead clients and only the contact fields actually supplied; deduplicate bookings and client identities; produce a private source-linked list with missing fields and coverage.

1. **Declare the scope.** Follow the [bookings and clients](chalet-bookings.walkthrough.md) method for actual-stay overlap with April 1–June 30 and supported attendance. Separate scheduled reservations lacking attendance confirmation. “Clients” means lead customers unless attendee identities are explicitly requested and supported. A party size does not provide the other guests' names or contacts.
2. **Discover and inventory.** Read the profile mapping, then list the chalet incoming reservation records, platform exports, and direct booking register in bounded batches. Include adjacent-quarter exports containing quarter-overlapping stays. Pair each original with its JSON sidecar using the actual schema. Search the authorized source set for missing fields; do not expand into unrelated personal records or external lookup. Payments and payouts corroborate a reservation but are not additional clients.
3. **Extract a booking-level ledger first.** Retain platform and reservation ID, stay dates, status, original client name, source customer ID if present, phone, email, contact type, source path and page/row. Check original pages when sidecars omit or conflict with a contact field. Record whether extraction and visual inspection were performed. Keep source spelling alongside any normalized name or phone; do not invent a country code or complete a partial number.

4. **Distinguish missing contact states.** “Show phone number,” masked values, buttons, and generated placeholders are not contact details. Say “not present in the supplied export” when the platform might hold a value that was not exported; do not assert the client has no phone. An explicitly blank direct-record field is “not supplied.” Preserve relay versus personal email labels; a relay address may expire and does not prove a personal address. Never infer contact type solely from an unfamiliar domain. Do not click through to retrieve further private details unless that access is already authorized and available. No external enrichment or outreach is part of extraction.
5. **Deduplicate cautiously.** Platform plus reservation ID removes repeated exports. The shared customer identifier establishes that A01/A02 belong to one lead client. Identical names alone do not justify merging people, especially across platforms. Retain ambiguous matches separately and explain possible duplicates; placeholders cannot identify one shared person. Retain all associated booking IDs and per-field citations when combining a client's rows.
6. **Write the private result and answer.** The example yields **3 bookings and 2 identified lead clients**. Both clients have a supplied email; one is explicitly a platform relay. No usable phone number is present in the supplied records. Return an on-demand private table; persist a separate export only when explicitly requested and authorized, with columns for client name, platform/customer ID, booking IDs, stay dates, phone, email, contact type, missing-field status, and source/page. Preserve the originals and canonical JSON. Include quarter basis, verification date, complete source inventory, unresolved identities, and absent source coverage. Do not copy real contact information into the public workflow or public benchmark evidence.

**Answer:** “The supplied Q2 stay records identify **2 lead clients across 3 completed bookings**. The private list contains their names, available emails, booking references, and source citations. One email is a platform relay; no usable phone numbers were included in the supplied records. Other guests' identities were not supplied.” Return the private table or link the authorized export; when authorized, retain its scoped summary in the one quarter summary under the [data FAQ cache contract](../flows/data-faq-cache.md).

**Recover:** if only scheduled reservations are available, label that list as scheduled clients and preserve attendance as unknown. If a source is inaccessible or lacks contacts, deliver the supported entries with explicit gaps rather than fabricating fields or treating a failed extraction as an empty client list.
