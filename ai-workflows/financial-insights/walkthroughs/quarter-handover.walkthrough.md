# Worked use case: accountant quarter handover

Fictional instructional example; all dates, paths, observations, and results are invented, not executed evidence.

**Request:** “What do I still need to collect for the accountant to close Q2 2027?”

**Plan:** produce an evidence-linked checklist for April 1–June 30 within the authorized organization/business scope. Previous closed-quarter checklists are a starting template; current activity and the accountant's supplied requirements establish applicability. Preparing a package does not itself certify accounting close or authorize sending it.

Follow the five stages in the [quarter-handover flow](../flows/quarter-handover.flow.md); the numbered decisions below illustrate them, not separate workflow roles. The Bookkeeper owns each stage; external approval remains external.

1. **Inspect: discover prior plans and current scope.** Read the latest applicable closed-quarter handover plan and its unresolved carry-forwards. Identify which businesses, accounts, cards, processors, loans, employees, and tax programs exist this quarter. Use the [account inventory](account-inventory.walkthrough.md); check for new and closed accounts. Do not inherit “not applicable” from a quarter before payroll or a new business began.
2. **Establish statement coverage first.** Build an interval matrix per account/source and verify that statements together cover the complete quarter, including statement cycles crossing quarter boundaries. A folder named June does not establish June 30 coverage. Track duplicate exports and missing pages. Bank/card statements anchor reconciliation, but do not establish purchase contents, business allocation, cash purchases, mileage, or all unpaid invoices.
3. **Process and collect before reconciliation.** The fictional inventory finds a phone receipt with an uncertain tax field and an unnormalized invoice without a sidecar. Resolve the supported canonical identity/destination and validate source-adjacent extraction separately through authorized routes. Keep the unreadable tax field review-pending, with reason and next action in the one summary; copying or renaming alone is not finished intake. Reuse unchanged valid pairs. For missing statement intervals, use the supported statements command; if an official Wise balance statement is missing, follow its command-owned [procedure](../../../ai-commands/data/statements/skills/wise-statements/SKILL.md), verifying account, currency and dates. New PDFs/images return to Process. A history export or provider download alone does not complete processing; unavailable capability leaves the item pending.
4. **Reconcile the applicable evidence.** Match outgoing charges to receipts, invoices, phone photos, refunds, and loan/tax documents; identify incoming deposits through sales invoices, bookings, platform settlement reports, fees, and refunds. Explain transfers without counting them as income or spending. Seek off-statement evidence for cash purchases, mileage, unpaid obligations, and other accountant-required schedules. Keep equipment and operating purchases distinguishable without inventing tax treatment.

   **Worked transfer case — client -> intermediary -> receiving bank:** Match the incoming intermediary receipt to the client invoice, then match the outgoing self-transfer to the receiving bank statement by date, currency, amount and transfer reference. Record the three legs as one linked movement: the client receipt is counted once as revenue, the intermediary fee is a separate cost candidate, and the transfer principal is excluded from revenue, salary and expense totals. If the receiving leg or a currency conversion is not evidenced, leave the chain review-pending and list that missing statement evidence; do not guess from the recipient name.
5. **Prepare handover status.** Use explicit states **complete**, **missing**, **needs review**, and **not applicable**. A checkbox is checked only for complete items, or for not-applicable items with a documented reason. Link every conclusion to evidence and name the next action for open items.

| Check | State | Fictional evidence or applicability | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [x] Operating-bank statements | Complete | Statement intervals cover April 1–June 30; pages checked | None |
| [ ] Credit-card statements | Missing | Last supplied statement ends June 20 | Obtain cycle covering June 21–30 |
| [ ] Purchase support | Needs review | Two confirmed charges have no itemized receipt | Request receipts; cite unmatched charges in the summary |
| [x] Booking income reconciliation | Complete | Reservation ledger, platform fees/refunds and bank payouts reconcile | Retain reconciliation and references |
| [ ] Mortgage breakdown | Missing | Bank cash payments known; lender allocation absent | Obtain principal/interest/component schedule |
| [ ] Payroll and remittances | Needs review | Payroll started this quarter; remittance settlement not verified | Reconcile payroll records and posted payments |
| [ ] Mileage | Missing | Cleaning-trip estimate exists without journey logs | Collect dashboard photos/logs or label estimate |
| [x] Cash purchases | Not applicable | Owner explicitly confirms none for this quarter, dated and retained | Reopen if new evidence appears |

6. **Answer with the actual open work.** “The package is not ready: collect late-cycle card coverage, two receipts, the mortgage allocation, and mileage evidence; reconcile payroll remittances. Bank statements and booking reconciliation are complete for their stated scope.” Do not mark the quarter complete because all currently supplied files were processed. Separate missing evidence, unresolved reconciliation, and accountant decisions.

For this example, all seven milestone boxes remain unchecked: collection is incomplete, reconciliation and questions remain open, and no readiness acceptance, delivery, accountant review or closure approval is observed. When the remaining evidence arrives, check each milestone only from its own proof. Delivery does not check review or closure; new contradictory evidence reopens affected items with reason/date.

**Recover and retain:** if a prior plan is absent, derive a provisional checklist from current activity and record the missing accountant requirements; do not invent proof of completeness. Keep the real checklist, source links, coverage, dated owner confirmations, open items and delivery references in the one `_common/qN/summary.md` through authorized effects under the [verified-answer contract](../flows/data-faq-cache.md). Apply the seven independent [milestones](../flows/record-organization.flow.md#quarter-status-checklist); granular collection checks are supporting detail, not readiness or closure. Compose normalized ledgers on demand from adjacent extracts rather than retaining duplicate combined files. Deliver or publish only through an authorized route, and leave final close acceptance to the accountant/human.
