# Worked use case: accountant quarter handover

Fictional instructional example; all dates, paths, observations, and results are invented, not executed evidence.

**Request:** “What do I still need to collect for the accountant to close Q2 2027?”

**Plan:** produce an evidence-linked checklist for April 1–June 30 within the authorized organization/business scope. Previous closed-quarter checklists are a starting template; current activity and the accountant's supplied requirements establish applicability. Preparing a package does not itself certify accounting close or authorize sending it.

1. **Discover prior plans and current scope.** Read the latest applicable closed-quarter handover plan and its unresolved carry-forwards. Identify which businesses, accounts, cards, processors, loans, employees, and tax programs exist this quarter. Use the [account inventory](account-inventory.walkthrough.md); check for new and closed accounts. Do not inherit “not applicable” from a quarter before payroll or a new business began.
2. **Establish statement coverage first.** Build an interval matrix per account/source and verify that statements together cover the complete quarter, including statement cycles crossing quarter boundaries. A folder named June does not establish June 30 coverage. Track duplicate exports and missing pages. Bank/card statements anchor reconciliation, but do not establish purchase contents, business allocation, cash purchases, mileage, or all unpaid invoices.
3. **Reconcile the applicable evidence.** Match outgoing charges to receipts, invoices, phone photos, refunds, and loan/tax documents; identify incoming deposits through sales invoices, bookings, platform settlement reports, fees, and refunds. Explain transfers without counting them as income or spending. Seek off-statement evidence for cash purchases, mileage, unpaid obligations, and other accountant-required schedules. Keep equipment and operating purchases distinguishable without inventing tax treatment.
4. **Build a reviewable checklist.** Use explicit states **complete**, **missing**, **needs review**, and **not applicable**. A checkbox is checked only for complete items, or for not-applicable items with a documented reason. Link every conclusion to evidence and name the next action for open items.

| Check | State | Fictional evidence or applicability | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [x] Operating-bank statements | Complete | Statement intervals cover April 1–June 30; pages checked | None |
| [ ] Credit-card statements | Missing | Last supplied statement ends June 20 | Obtain cycle covering June 21–30 |
| [ ] Purchase support | Needs review | Two confirmed charges have no itemized receipt | Request receipts; retain unmatched charge ledger |
| [x] Booking income reconciliation | Complete | Reservation ledger, platform fees/refunds and bank payouts reconcile | Retain reconciliation and references |
| [ ] Mortgage breakdown | Missing | Bank cash payments known; lender allocation absent | Obtain principal/interest/component schedule |
| [ ] Payroll and remittances | Needs review | Payroll started this quarter; remittance settlement not verified | Reconcile payroll records and posted payments |
| [ ] Mileage | Missing | Cleaning-trip estimate exists without journey logs | Collect dashboard photos/logs or label estimate |
| [x] Cash purchases | Not applicable | Owner explicitly confirms none for this quarter, dated and retained | Reopen if new evidence appears |

5. **Answer with the actual open work.** “The package is not ready: collect late-cycle card coverage, two receipts, the mortgage allocation, and mileage evidence; reconcile payroll remittances. Bank statements and booking reconciliation are complete for their stated scope.” Do not mark the quarter complete because all currently supplied files were processed. Separate missing evidence, unresolved reconciliation, and accountant decisions.

**Recover and cache:** if a prior plan is absent, derive a provisional checklist from current activity and record the missing accountant requirements; do not invent proof of completeness. Store the real checklist, normalized ledgers, source links, coverage, dated owner confirmations, open items, and package manifest with authorized quarter data. Cache the question and status under the [data FAQ contract](../flows/data-faq-cache.md). Deliver or publish only through an authorized route, and leave final close acceptance to the accountant/human.
