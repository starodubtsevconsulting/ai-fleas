# On Writing Well

Reusable blogging method inspired by William Zinsser's *On Writing Well*.

This is an interpretation of general writing principles, not a reproduction of the book.

## Intent

Make prose easy to read by removing everything that does not earn its place.

## Apply

- Prefer simple, familiar words when they carry the meaning.
- Prefer concrete nouns and active verbs.
- Remove clutter, repetition, inflated transitions, unnecessary qualifiers, jargon, and decorative abstraction.
- Keep sentences as short as the thought allows, but vary rhythm naturally.
- Make each paragraph do one useful job.
- Preserve the author's voice; simplicity should sound human, not standardized.
- Explain technical ideas in ordinary language before introducing specialized vocabulary when possible.
- Cut a sentence or paragraph when the article remains equally clear and alive without it.

## Review question

Can this be said more simply without losing meaning, evidence, personality, or necessary precision?
