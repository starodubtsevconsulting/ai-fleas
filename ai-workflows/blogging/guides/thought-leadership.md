# Thought-leadership guide

Build a coherent body of original perspective in areas where the author has experience, evidence, or a defensible point
of view.

- Prefer firsthand experience, concrete observations, and developed arguments over generic content volume.
- Publish when there is something worth saying while maintaining enough continuity for themes to develop.
- Connect posts into recurring themes so the archive becomes an intellectual asset.
- Preserve the author's voice and uncertainty where appropriate.
- Use research to strengthen or challenge an argument, not to manufacture one.
- Treat disagreement, reader questions, and real-world outcomes as evidence that may refine later thinking.
- Repeat an earlier argument only when new evidence or a materially different perspective justifies it.

Typical flow: `experience or idea -> relate to themes -> develop viewpoint -> research -> challenge -> draft -> approve -> publish -> learn`.
