# Search-informed-content guide

Develop useful articles around demonstrated audience questions or discovery demand without creating material merely to
satisfy keywords.

- Start from a real audience question or search intent.
- Research the topic and existing coverage before selecting an angle.
- Add information gain through evidence, explanation, examples, or perspective.
- Match structure and depth to reader intent instead of maximizing length or keyword frequency.
- Maintain factual provenance and editorial voice.
- Observe discovery evidence after publication and update or consolidate only when evidence warrants it.
- Avoid near-duplicate pages aimed only at minor keyword variants.

Typical flow: `audience evidence -> opportunity -> intent and coverage research -> angle -> draft -> review -> approve -> publish -> observe -> update or follow up`.
