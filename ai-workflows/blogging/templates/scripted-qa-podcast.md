# Scripted Q&A / podcast-style article

Use for a concise public article presented as a fictional or clearly framed interviewer/guest conversation to explain one idea through questions and answers.

## Audience contract

- Broad professional reader; keep questions natural and answers self-contained.
- Target approximately a 2–4 minute read unless the brief requires otherwise.
- Dialogue is an editorial structure, not a claim that a real interview occurred.

## Shape

`opening question -> practical experience/problem -> follow-up questions that expose the idea -> tradeoffs/evidence -> useful conclusion`

Questions should move the argument forward rather than merely divide prose into sections. Answers may contain personal experience when true, but do not invent a speaker, interview, quotation, or event.

## Constraints

- Clearly disclose fictional/scripted voices when they are not real people.
- Keep one central idea; avoid repetitive interviewer prompts.
- Preserve uncertainty and distinguish experience from general claims.
- Prefer natural spoken language without filler or fake podcast theatrics.
- Finish when the reader's main question is answered.

## Acceptance

The reader should understand what problem was explored, what changed in the author's understanding, the practical tradeoff, and what they could try or consider next.
