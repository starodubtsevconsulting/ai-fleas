# Book-writing workflow

Use this workflow for explicitly requested work that develops an idea, research, lived experience, or a partial
manuscript into a coherent book while preserving author intent, voice, factual integrity, and continuity.

## Strategy selection

- [Outline first](guides/outline-first.md) when argument order, plot architecture, coverage, or dependencies benefit from
  early structure.
- [Discovery writing](guides/discovery-writing.md) when voice, ideas, characters, or structure should emerge through
  drafting.

Strategies may be combined or changed as evidence from the manuscript develops.

## Required behavior

- Treat manuscripts, research sources, and recorded human decisions as authoritative artifacts.
- Preserve provenance and distinguish sourced facts, interpretation, invention, and placeholders.
- Preserve the author's voice and intent; do not fabricate lived experience or silently normalize the prose.
- Track continuity, accepted structure, open questions, and rejected directions only within profile-authorized artifacts.
- Require explicit authorization before publishing, distributing, or overwriting a manuscript.
- Load only the portion of the manuscript and research needed for the current task.

Typical flow: `premise and audience -> select strategy -> research and structure -> draft -> continuity and factual review -> revise -> human approval -> publish`.

The selected profile may define private manuscript locations, voice guidance, audiences, and destinations. The host
supplies editing, storage, and publishing mechanics without changing this workflow.
