# Why AI Fleas

AI Fleas keeps reusable AI behavior independent of any one host. Its commands, workflows, roles, governance, and profile
contract can be loaded directly by a compatible AI application or consumed by an optional companion implementation.

This separation lets people share and improve common rules without publishing private projects, credentials, providers,
runtime state, launcher code, or application mechanics. A selected profile supplies the working context and scope; a host
supplies execution mechanics without silently changing the rules.
