# LinkedIn Article schedule

Use only as Release Coordinator when the selected LinkedIn destination enables scheduling and the exact revision has passed its LinkedIn-specific review plus human acceptance/session mandate.

1. Verify account, accepted archive revision, intended LinkedIn Article representation, destination review evidence and configured release policy.
2. Inspect current LinkedIn publishing/scheduling UI and existing known queue/history. Do not rely on saved coordinates.
3. Choose a future slot under the LinkedIn destination's own policy; Medium cadence does not transfer.
4. Use LinkedIn's native scheduling control only when the live UI clearly identifies the intended Article and future date/time. Never choose immediate Publish as a fallback.
5. Read back scheduled state/date/time and URL/identity. Return evidence for Writer archive update. If uncertain, stop and inspect before retrying to avoid duplicate release.

No API or paid account is assumed. Authentication comes from the authorized existing browser session.
