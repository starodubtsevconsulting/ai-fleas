# Why <Command Name>?

Explain the human reason this command exists.

Focus on the problem or outcome, not implementation details. A reader should understand why the command is worth having
before reading its AI contract or executable code.

Include, where relevant:

- the problem being solved;
- why it matters;
- what control, capability, time, safety, quality, or simplicity the command provides;
- important limitations or tradeoffs;
- why this belongs as a reusable AI command rather than a one-off procedure.

Keep this file human-facing. Execution rules, routing, inputs, safety contracts, and completion criteria belong in the
command contract/specification.
