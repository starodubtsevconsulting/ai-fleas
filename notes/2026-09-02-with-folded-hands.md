# With Folded Hands

**September 2, 2026**

I was talking to someone in sales about using AI to automate more of his work.

His first concern was trust: *If a client is worth millions, do I really want an AI writing to them?* Fair point. Sometimes spending your own time on someone is part of the relationship.

But then he said something more interesting.

*I worked through ten leads today. I felt like I accomplished something. If AI does all of that, what is left for me to do?*

It made me think of Jack Williamson's *With Folded Hands...*

It seems the question might be shifting from **what can we automate?** to **what do we still want to do ourselves?**

> “What stands in the way becomes the way.”  
> — Marcus Aurelius
