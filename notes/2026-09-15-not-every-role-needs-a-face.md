# Not Every Role Needs a Face

**September 15, 2026**

Today's insight after spending too much time organizing AI agents:

Maybe not every role needs to be a persistent agent.

Human-facing agents need continuity. But a Coder? Give it the task, the spec, the right context, let it code, and let it disappear.

The workflow persists. The evidence persists. The Coder doesn't have to.

So apparently the Coder is a faceless agent.

Who would have thought.
