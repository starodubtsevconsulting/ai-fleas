# Why AI Fleas?

**September 4, 2026**

I remembered an old anecdote about a student preparing for a zoology exam. He had learned only one subject well: fleas.

Asked about a dog, he quickly got to its fur — and then to fleas. Asked about another animal, somehow the answer again ended up with fleas. Even when asked about a fish, he found a way back to fleas.

It feels a little like conversations today.

It doesn't seem to matter much what people do — software, sales, art, finance, education. Sooner or later, somehow, everyone starts talking about AI.

AI has become our fleas.

Some may also hear **AI Fleas** as **AI fleets** — many AIs working together. That fits rather well too.

So today, while walking in the park and talking to AI through voice-to-speech, I decided to rename this repository **AI Fleas**.

A small historical moment for the project.
